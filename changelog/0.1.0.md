Initial release.

- Added the energy-powered Repair Station with five equipment slots.
- Repairs normal durability-based tools, weapons, and armor without changing enchantments or other item data.
- Repairs are shared fairly between slots; energy storage, cost, speed, and allowed items are configurable.
- Supports redstone pause and hopper/compatible-pipe input and output. Automated extraction only removes fully repaired items.
- Recipe uses four iron ingots, two redstone dust, one anvil, and two Mending I enchanted books.
- Includes the Repair Station menu with inventory transfers, repair status, and an energy display.
- Supports Forge 1.12.2, 1.16.5, 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1, 26.1.2, 26.2. OpenUI MC 0.0.16+ is required on clients.
