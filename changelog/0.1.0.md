Initial alpha development version.

- One energy-powered repair station with five equipment slots.
- Station recipe: four iron ingots, two redstone dust, one anvil and two Mending I enchanted books.
- Shared repair behavior across Forge 1.12.2, 1.16.5, 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1, 26.1.2, 26.2.
- Configurable energy usage, repair rate, capacity and item filters.
- Shared wrench-and-lightning project icon in every mod listing, plus a CurseForge front-page description.
- Custom repair-workbench model using four ImageGen materials resized to 16×16, with placement facing and a dedicated creative tab.
- OpenUI screen with five repair slots, player inventory/hotbar, shift-click transfers, live status and energy tooltip; requires OpenUI MC 0.0.16+ on clients.
- Redstone pause, persistent inventory and energy, and hopper/pipe automation.
